how it works

From a fingertip to a redrawn problem, in four steps.

You just do math out loud. The tutor picks the picture.

1.

Sense

A fingertip sensor streams your body signals.

2.

Calibrate

A short calm stretch sets your baseline.

3.

Decide

Too much load? One step more concrete.

4.

Re-draw

The same question comes back, redrawn.
